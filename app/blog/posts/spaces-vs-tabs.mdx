# ⚖️ JurisAI

**JurisAI** is an AI-assisted legal research platform that analyzes case documents and surfaces likely outcomes, built solo end to end on the MERN stack.

## Key Features

- **Document Analysis:**
  Parses legal documents and extracts structured insights to speed up case review.

- **Case Outcome Prediction:**
  Applies AI models to historical case data to estimate case probabilities.

- **Collaborative Workspace:**
  Multi-user dashboard for legal teams to share insights and work cases together.

## Tech Stack

React (frontend), Node.js/Express (backend), MongoDB (data layer).
