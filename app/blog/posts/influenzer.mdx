# ◆ Influenzer

**Influenzer** is the backend for a B2B creator marketplace connecting brands and content creators — brands manage campaigns and discover creators, creators browse a job feed and submit proposals.

---

## 🔍 Overview

### Architecture

```
Brand → Campaign → Creator → Proposal → Escrow → Payout
```

- **Auth:** email/password and Google OAuth.
- **Marketplace:** brand-side campaign management and creator discovery, creator-side job feed and proposals.
- **Media:** video uploads to AWS S3.
- **Messaging:** real-time communication between brands and creators.
- **Payments:** Razorpay-backed escrow and wallet tracking for campaign payouts.
- **Architecture:** Clean Architecture in Go (Gin Gonic, GORM, PostgreSQL on Neon), containerized with Docker, documented with Swagger.
