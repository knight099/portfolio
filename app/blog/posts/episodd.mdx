# 🎬 Episodd

**Episodd** is a mobile-first OTT platform for bite-sized (1–2 min) web series, currently MVP launched and in pre-launch phase at [episodd.com](https://episodd.com).

---

## 🔍 Overview

### Key Features:
- **Short-Form Streaming:**
  Bite-sized (1–2 min) web series designed for mobile-first consumption.

- **Creator Monetization:**
  Creator monetization tools with a **70% revenue share model**.

- **Subscription Billing:**
  Built-in subscription billing for premium content.

- **Offline Downloads:**
  Support for offline downloads and multi-language content delivery.

- **Admin & Creator Dashboards:**
  Dedicated dashboards for creators and admins with analytics.

### Tech Stack:
Flutter (app), Next.js/React (web), Go microservices backend, PostgreSQL (NeonDB), AWS.
